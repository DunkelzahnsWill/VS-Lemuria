# Patch: the smoke test's Motor check reads the quern's fastest speed since the Motor started (G43, feature 8)

**Done.** `MachinesTests`: the Motor scene starts a 200 ms watch on the quern's mechanical network, and motorcheck
takes the fastest speed seen since then as well as the speed now, then ends the watch. Nothing outside the smoke
test changes.

**Root cause.** motorcheck read the network's speed once, seven console commands (about seven seconds) after the
Motor started. The game's mechanical network spins up over its own ticks, and on a busy runner, where the heavy
scenes between the two commands hold the server, it had not yet left zero at that one reading (G37's run at 06:39Z;
the next run on the same commit passed). The Motor itself was powered and joined in that run's earlier line. A
watch over the whole spin-up asks the same question, "did the quern turn", without resting on one moment.

**Why not another way.** Waiting longer before motorcheck would push the rod tap later and squeeze motorstopped's
check behind it; a peak keeps both where they are. The check still fails if the quern never turns.

**Checks.** setup, build and smoke test pass ("the quern turns: yes").
