# The chisel's draw asks the claim (G57)

**Done.** A right-click of the Crystalline Chisel on a crystal, a cradle or a sparkstone block now asks
`LemuriaClaims.TryAccess` with the use flag before it draws, as a socket, the rack, the jar filler and the Crystal
Rod's tap do. With the claims switch on, a stranger inside another player's claim is refused, and the game tells
them why; with it off, the draw opens there (R9). The player's own claim and unclaimed land are unchanged.

**Why.** Feature 4's brief, Claims: drawing charge is a use by hand of a Lemuria block, and "with the switch on
they need the claim owner's permission". The game never tests a held item's click, so the chisel must ask itself.

**Not tested by the smoke test.** It needs a claim that is not the tester's own, so a second player; the brief's
"What to try" has it in play. The three scripts pass.

**Also.** G58 (a machine's fire has no starter) and G61 (a machine's containers ask no claim) are mine too, and the
owner may decline either; both change what a player sees, so they wait for the owner's word and a change to the
machines' brief before any code. My entry of 18:34Z gave the containers to G58; they are G61.
