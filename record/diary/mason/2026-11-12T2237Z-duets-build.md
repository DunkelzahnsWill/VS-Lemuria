**Choices the brief left open (rule 1's exception).** Each is on the PR's "what to try" list where the player sees it.

1. A Duet Module set into a Focus that holds a crystal, a third crystal into the module, and a second crystal into a
   drain with a Memory are refused with the dull sound every refused socket makes. A Focus holding a module that is not
   full says "The Duet Module holds 1 of 2 crystals".
2. A drain that already holds two crystals when a Memory is set against it works as before, and its Memory sits still,
   until one crystal is taken out.
3. A Focus with a Duet Module and no pair in its Memory works nothing: its module's crystals are for duets only.
4. Clay is made in the block just in front of the Focus, as the brief says "the block the Focus faces"; a machine's
   ray starts inside that block and would pass it by.
5. The pair swaps with the module's after every duet working, a miss included (what to try 5 needs it), but not after
   one the network could not pay.
6. Fired clay takes only what the pit kiln fires one for one (every raw pot, brick and tile in the game).
7. An Earth crystal's turn on a drain also ends after a look in which it paid stored charge back, so Sky comes round
   while the battery still holds a count.
8. Panned sand loses an even share of what is stored: the count divided by the room's weak blocks.
9. One whole block of sand is eight pannings, as the game's pan takes one layer of a full block a time
   (`pannedBlock` `sand-{rock}-7`); each rolls the pan's drops as the pan does. No player's drop rate applies.
10. A shelf against a drain does not take the drain's finds: they go to the container touching the drain, as the brief
    says.
11. `BloomPlantsPerWorking` sits in the Machines' settings file, since Machines reads it; the two duet settings sit in
    `Lemuria/Duets/`.
