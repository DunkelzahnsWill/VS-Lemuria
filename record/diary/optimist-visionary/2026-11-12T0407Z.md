# 2026-11-12 04:07Z: the change to the rules that adds the precursor (version 3)

**What was done.** record/COMMANDMENTS.txt changes in two places and nowhere else.
1. The head of the file. "THE COMMANDMENTS, version 2" becomes "version 3", and the line under it becomes "Ratified
   2026-11-12. Replaces version 2 (2026-11-11)."
2. The cast, fourth tier. A third entry, "The precursor", is added after the inquisitor's. Its words are the
   paragraph the owner said yes to in R17. I copied it by program from the message the owner read, so nothing is
   added and nothing is left out.

**Why.** In R15 the owner chose "Keeper of the old paper, no code" for the Precursor thread, and the rules had no
such role. By rule 1 a ruling that changes the rules binds from its entry, and the planning thread writes the change
at once. R17 is the owner's word for these exact words (rule 4).

**Why not another way.**
- Why not leave the rules alone and name the precursor only in the Road Bible: the cast is where a thread gets its
  office and its limits ("Rank settles who may change what"), and the rules name every other kind of thread. A
  thread known only to the Road Bible would hold the old notes with no rule that keeps it from changing them, and no
  rule that says which stands where an old note and the record differ.
- Why not a builder that owns no features, like the proto thread and the janitor: both of those exist to change
  code. The precursor's whole office is that it changes nothing.
- Why the fourth tier: the precursor decides nothing and gates nothing, so it does not stand beside the planning
  thread and the review thread. I put the tier to the owner as my own addition, and the owner said yes.
- Why no other sentence changes: the owner said yes to one paragraph. Rule 5 already gives every thread a diary
  folder named for its role and has every thread read the rules when it starts, and the record already has a
  thread's answers go in its own diary. So the precursor needs no further words to keep its diary
  (record/diary/precursor/) or to be started and stopped like any thread.
- The date in the head: 2026-11-12 is the day of the owner's word, R17, by the clock the record keeps, which is UTC.
  Where the owner lives it was still 2026-11-11. Why not the day of the merge: I cannot know it when I write, and a
  wrong date in the rules could only be put right by another change to the rules.

**What the change leaves to the Road Bible.** The paragraph has the Road Bible's shared list name the old paper and
say where it lies. Lemuria has no shared list yet: it is part of the first Road Bible, my next work. Until that
merges, R5, R8 and R15 say what the old paper is: the old notes, the roadmap and the old diaries.

**For the review thread, which reads this before it merges (rule 6 e).**

  brought into line now, and a reconciliation comes at a later time. R18 goes to main by a record-only PR of its own.
- I found no code on main that this change makes wrong. It adds one role and takes nothing from any other. That is
  my reading only: the list is the review thread's to make (rule 4).
