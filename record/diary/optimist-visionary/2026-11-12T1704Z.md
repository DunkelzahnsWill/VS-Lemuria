# 2026-11-12 17:04Z: the change to the concept for G40, the last sentence of standing rule 7 (the claims switch)

**Why this entry.** The review thread's finding G40 is a flaw of mine in the concept of the first Road Bible: the last
sentence of standing rule 7 says "by hand", and in this book those two words are the mark of a naked evocation. This
is its correction, and this entry rides in its PR (rule 5). What I read on starting at 16:52Z is in my entry of
17:02Z, which rides in the PR for part 3.

**What was done.** One sentence of part 1 changes, the last of standing rule 7, and nothing else in the book. "What a
player breaks or places by hand inside another player's claim stays as the game rules it, whatever the switch says:"
becomes "The game's own breaking and placing of blocks inside another player's claim stay as the game rules them,
whatever the switch says:". The rest of the sentence stays as it was: "the owner's words open Lemuria's blocks there
to use and to nothing more (the card of 2026-11-10, and R9)." The file keeps its 548 lines, and three of them differ.
A program of mine made the change. It refuses unless the old words stand in standing rule 7 exactly once, and I ran it
without writing first, against the book as main has it.

**What was wrong.** The sentence was new with the second commit of PR #16, and it answered the third point of G25:
what the switch opens when it is off. I wrote "by hand" to keep the game's own breaking and placing apart from
Lemuria's workings, which with the switch off no longer spare a claim (my entry of 06:29Z). In this book the two words
do not do that. A naked evocation is an alignment's "single use by hand" and its "one working by hand", and feature 6
is "Casting by hand". And an evocation breaks and places blocks: Tremor "raises a pillar or mound". So the sentence
could be read two ways, as G40 sets out. In the first it speaks of the game's own breaking and placing of blocks,
about which Lemuria's code is never asked. In the second it speaks of everything that a player does in person, a naked
evocation included, and then only what machines do would follow the switch.

**Which reading the record holds.** The first, on every count that G40 names. The rule's own first sentence: "Every
land claim rule in Lemuria goes through one switch, so that none can drift". The head of the standing rules, by which
one sentence among them is new with the book, and it is another. The old paper, as G40 quotes the note of old step 3:
"One switch covers every claim rule in Lemuria, from evocations to the chisel, racks and jars". The code, in which G40
finds five places that ask the switch before a player's own act changes a block. And my entry of 06:29Z. The first
reading is what I meant, and it is what is built.

**Why these words.** They are the review thread's, from G40: name the game's own breaking and placing, and drop "by
hand". "The game's own" says whose rule it is. The game's claims rule the game's own breaking and placing of blocks,
and Lemuria's switch is never asked about them. A naked evocation, the Crystalline Chisel in a player's hand and a
machine are Lemuria's workings, and the sentence before says of those that they follow the switch.

**What it does not change.** Nothing that is built, and no brief, because none is on main yet. The sentence before it
keeps "every Lemuria block that is used by hand". Those words follow the question that R9 answers, they speak of a
block that a player uses, and G40 does not name them.

**Why not another way.**
- *Leave the sentence out.* It answers the third point of G25. Without it the book would not say where the opening
  ends when the switch is off.
- *Keep "by hand" and say what it means here.* Standing rule 3 asks for one meaning to a word, and "by hand" has its
  meaning in this book already.
- *List what follows the switch.* The sentence before says it of Lemuria's workings as a whole. A list is for the
  briefs to give, feature by feature, and G40 names the four that must: features 4, 6, 8 and 9.
- *Wait for the owner's command.* Rule 4 lets the concept change by the owner's command as well. Then the owner would
  have to give the words. In a PR the owner reads the one sentence and merges, and PR #16's description told the owner
  that this change would come that way.

**How this change merges.** It is a change to the concept: the review thread reads it, and then the owner merges it
(rule 4). It is never set to auto-merge, and it is assigned to the owner only once its description cites the review
thread's report on it. The changed sentence cites R9, which I recorded as deputy, so rule 4 holds it for the owner's
merge on that count as well. When it merges, the coordinator tells every thread, and each reads the Road Bible again
before it goes on (rule 5).

**When.** G40 asks for it before the briefs of features 4, 6, 8 and 9 are put up. At 17:03Z no PR was open in the
repo, so no brief is put up yet.

**The checks.** The three scripts are run on the commit that carries this entry, before the PR opens, and the PR's
description says what they showed.

**The PR.** From the branch road-bible/concept-standing-rule-7 (R16) into main. I tell the coordinator once it is
