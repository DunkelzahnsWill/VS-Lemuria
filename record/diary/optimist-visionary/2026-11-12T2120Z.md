**Why these words.**
- *The magazine, and not Memory as a block.* The hold is the magazine's. The crystal in the machine's socket stands in
  the open, which is what lets Earth and Sky take turns. Mason's brief has it so in each place where it speaks of it
  (PR #135 at d3178e9). The duets' brief, item 10: "a crystal in the magazine never dialogues". The machines' brief,
  item 18: "The magazine keeps its crystals out of dialogues up to Glowing". The crystals' brief, item 8, of what
  never dialogues up to Glowing: "nor one in a Memory's magazine". The book's sentence takes the same words.
- *The gloss, "the module that holds further crystals for a machine".* Part 1 names Memory nowhere else, so a reader
  meets it here first. The gloss is true of both things that Memory does: it cycles a machine's crystals (feature 8),
  and it lends a pair for a duet (feature 10).
- *Why the owner's word is not in the sentence.* The owner calls Memory a "Shielded" block, and sets the limit beside
  the word: up to Glowing. In the book the shielded jar is the one thing that holds a crystal back from Luminous up,
  and the next sentence says so with "only". So the sentence says what the owner's word means for Memory in the words
  that the book already has for jars and racks, and it cites R32, where the owner's word stands. Whether the mod's own
  text calls Memory shielded is for the briefs of features 8 and 10 to settle (standing rule 3), and the review thread
  takes the word up in its read of the brief (G87). If the owner wants the word in the book as well, I add it.
- *The next sentence stays as it is:* "From Luminous up only a *shielded jar* holds a crystal back." It is true while
  the magazine's hold ends at Glowing, and that is the owner's word and the brief's.
