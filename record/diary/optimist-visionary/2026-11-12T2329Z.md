> The record thread keeps for the coordinator a copy of rulings.md and a copy of findings.md as well, in the same
> place, and all that the paragraph above says of a copy of a book holds for a copy of either file, except that the

**Why so.**
- *When the coordinator reads.* I took "before it acts on it" from version 6's sentence on a thread that is sent an
  entry by its number alone. Such a thread is sent what concerns it, so it knows which entry to read before which act.
  The coordinator is told of every entry, and what an entry asks of it shows only in the reading: the review thread
  names G96, which asks a note with step 1's zip. Under version 6 each entry reached the coordinator word for word, so
  it had read each when it came. The owner's yes was to a copy that ends that sending (R37). Nobody asked the owner
  whether the coordinator may read later, and my first text would have let it.
- *Why these words.* "before it goes on" is what rules 4 and 5 say of a reading that waits for nothing: "each reads
  them again before it goes on", of the rules, and "each reads the Road Bible again before it goes on". So the
  coordinator reads an entry when it is told of it, as it did when the entry came by message.
- *Against what an entry is new.* My first text left that open. My entry of 23:07Z read a first copy as all new, and
  only the looser reading made that bearable: findings.md has 5,983 lines today. Now the measure is what the
  coordinator has had: an entry "that the coordinator has not yet been sent or told of". What reached it word for word
  under version 6 counts as sent, so a first copy names only what came after the last sending, and every later copy
  names what the coordinator has not been told of.
- *What has changed since.* The second sentence ends "and of each that has changed since", which keeps what "or
  changed" was there for. An entry can gain a line after it is written, as R35 gained its line "Closed", and the
  coordinator is then told of it again.
- *Why not count from the copy before.* A first copy has none before it, and the two files get their copies at
  different merges, so there would be two counts to keep. What the coordinator has had is one measure for both files,
  and it holds as well if a copy is ever lost and made again.
- *Why the sentence names the copy and the coordinator outright.* An earlier draft of mine had a pronoun in that place
  which could be read two ways.
- *What my entry of 23:07Z says that no longer holds.* Two of its points under "What I had to settle": the one on how
  an entry now reaches the coordinator, where it calls every entry of a first copy new, and the one headed "The
  coordinator reads before it acts". Its blockquote of the paragraph is the first commit's. Everything else in it
  stands.
- *What this commit leaves as it was.* The head, the changed sentence of the record thread's paragraph, the first and
  the fourth sentence of the new paragraph, and the row in the Road Bible.

**The owner's yes covers it as before** (R37). The yes names the copy. These two sentences say only how the copy is
